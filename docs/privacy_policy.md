# Privacy Policy (Draft)

Last updated: 2026-05-08

This Privacy Policy explains how PublicDomainJazzPlayer (the "App") handles information.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to the App and its related catalog delivery endpoints used by the App.

## 2. Data We Collect

At this stage, the App is designed to operate with minimal data collection.

- We do **not** require account registration.
- We do **not** collect direct identifiers such as name, email address, or phone number through the App UI.
- We do **not** request precise location permission.

## 3. Data Stored on Device

The App may store limited local app data to improve resilience and UX:

- Cached catalog JSON (for fallback when remote fetch fails)
- Local app state needed for playback/session behavior

This local data remains on your device unless the app is uninstalled or app data is cleared.

## 4. Network Requests

The App makes requests to:

- Catalog JSON endpoints (for track metadata)
- External audio URLs (for playback)
- External source/rights links when users explicitly open them

These requests are required for app functionality.

## 5. Third-Party Sources and Links

The App may direct users to third-party websites (for example, source and rights references).  
Those services have their own privacy practices and policies.

## 6. Analytics and Tracking

The current implementation does not include dedicated user analytics or advertising SDKs.

If analytics or telemetry is added in the future, this policy will be updated before release.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children. If legal requirements apply in your jurisdiction, additional disclosures may be added.

## 8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is published in this repository.

## 9. Contact

For inquiries, use the repository issue tracker.
